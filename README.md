## What this Marketplace does

This automated marketing console takes your restaurant website and automatically generates:
1. **Launch & Food Promo Cards:** High-resolution pictures for Instagram feeds, Facebook, and LinkedIn featuring Dum Biryani, BBQ Sizzlers, Malai Boti, Shinwari Karahi, and special deals.
2. **Short Launch Reels / TikTok / Shorts (25s):** Fast, appetizing vertical video with traditional lounge music highlighting your restaurant's best dishes and ordering link.
3. **Full Restaurant Tour Video:** Automatically scrolls through your website (`Home`, `Menu`, `Order`, `Track Order`, `Reviews`).
4. **Buffer & Cloudflare Scheduling:** Schedules a full week of daily posts to Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, and X in one click.

## How to use it (Quick Start)

### 1. Double-click `Start.cmd`
- Simply go to `Desktop\marketplace` and double-click `Start.cmd`.
- It starts the server on `http://localhost:4545` and opens your browser automatically!
- Keep the black terminal window open while you use it.

### 2. Connect Tab
- Paste your **Buffer API key** (linked with `sekhawatarain79@gmail.com`).
- Paste your **Cloudflare Pages token** (project name: `zaiqa-media-files`).
- Click **Check and save**.

### 3. Project Tab
- **Zaiqa Royal Grill** is already pre-configured!
- You can also click **Read my project** with `http://localhost:5173` to take live screenshots of your restaurant site.

### 4. Make & Schedule Tab
- Click **Make and schedule a week** or make single pictures, reels, and stories.
- All media is automatically uploaded and scheduled on your social media channels.
